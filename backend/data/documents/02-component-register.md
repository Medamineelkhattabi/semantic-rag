# Helios Aerospace — Component Register

Document ID: DOC-COMP-2027
Classification: Internal
Owner: Chief Engineer's Office
Last revised: 2027-03-28

## Scope

The Component Register is the authoritative record of every qualified flight
hardware component, its technical designation, its qualification status and the
supplier responsible for its manufacture and delivery. Programme allocation is
not recorded here; see the Project Portfolio for programme dependencies.

## C-17 — Inertial Navigation Module

The C-17 is a three-axis strapdown inertial navigation module built around a
ring-laser gyroscope assembly. It provides attitude and position reference at
100 Hz with a drift specification of 0.008 degrees per hour.

- Designation: INM-3A
- Qualification status: Qualified (2026-11-14)
- Unit cost: EUR 41,500
- Lead time: 22 weeks

Component C-17 is supplied by Alpha Precision Systems. Alpha Precision Systems
is the sole qualified manufacturer of the C-17 module; the second-source
qualification programme was suspended in 2025 on cost grounds and has not been
reinstated.

## C-22 — Power Regulator Unit

The C-22 is a 28 VDC regulated power distribution unit with triple-redundant
current limiting and built-in transient suppression.

- Designation: PRU-880
- Qualification status: Qualified (2026-06-02)
- Unit cost: EUR 12,300
- Lead time: 14 weeks

Component C-22 is supplied by Borealis Components GmbH.

## C-31 — Cryogenic Valve Assembly

The C-31 is a cryogenic shutoff and throttling valve assembly rated for liquid
hydrogen service at 20 K with a 400-cycle qualification life.

- Designation: CVA-12
- Qualification status: Qualified (2026-09-19)
- Unit cost: EUR 88,700
- Lead time: 31 weeks

Component C-31 is supplied by Cygnus Aerospace Ltd.

## C-45 — Ka-Band Transceiver

The C-45 is a Ka-band uplink and downlink transceiver providing 1.2 Gbps
aggregate throughput with adaptive coding and modulation.

- Designation: KBT-900
- Qualification status: Qualified (2027-01-08)
- Unit cost: EUR 63,200
- Lead time: 18 weeks

Component C-45 is supplied by Delta Microwave Inc.

## C-58 — Thermal Shield Panel

The C-58 is an ablative thermal protection panel for base-region heat shielding,
using a phenolic-impregnated carbon ablator on a titanium honeycomb substrate.

- Designation: TSP-450
- Qualification status: Conditionally qualified (2026-12-03)
- Unit cost: EUR 27,900
- Lead time: 26 weeks

Component C-58 is supplied by Cygnus Aerospace Ltd. The conditional
qualification reflects an open action on bondline void fraction that remains
unclosed as of this revision.

## C-63 — Flight Data Recorder

The C-63 is a crash-survivable flight data recorder with 128 GB of solid-state
storage and a 30-day underwater locator beacon.

- Designation: FDR-256
- Qualification status: Qualified (2026-08-21)
- Unit cost: EUR 19,800
- Lead time: 12 weeks

Component C-63 is supplied by Alpha Precision Systems.

## C-77 — Ground Antenna Servo

The C-77 is a two-axis antenna pointing servo drive for 7.3 m ground station
reflectors, with 0.02-degree pointing accuracy under 90 km/h wind loading.

- Designation: GAS-73
- Qualification status: Qualified (2027-02-11)
- Unit cost: EUR 34,600
- Lead time: 20 weeks

Component C-77 is supplied by Delta Microwave Inc.

## Register notes

Supplier entries in this register name the responsible manufacturing
organisation only. The commercial instrument governing each supplier
relationship, together with any risk exposure attached to that instrument, is
recorded separately in the Supplier Directory, the Contract Register and the
Risk Register.
