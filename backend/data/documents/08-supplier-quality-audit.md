# Supplier Quality Audit Report — Alpha Precision Systems

Document ID: DOC-AUD-ALPHA-2027
Classification: Internal — Commercial in Confidence
Owner: Quality Assurance
Audit dates: 2027-03-16 to 2027-03-18
Lead auditor: David Kimani

## Executive summary

An on-site process and systems audit of Alpha Precision Systems was conducted at
the Grenoble facility over three days. The audit was triggered by the incoming
inspection findings recorded earlier in the year and by the supplier's B rating
under continued monitoring.

The audit closed with two major non-conformances and four minor observations.
The supplier's quality management system remains certified, but the audit team
does not recommend lifting the enhanced incoming inspection regime until both
major non-conformances are verified closed.

## Major non-conformance NCR-2027-011 — Change control

The supplier modified the thermal soak profile used during gyroscope assembly
without raising a change notification to Helios, contrary to the change-control
obligations in the governing long-term supply agreement. The modification is the
probable root cause of the calibration drift observed at incoming inspection.

- Required action: reinstate notification workflow, retrospectively notify all
  process changes made since 2026-07-01
- Due: 2027-05-31

## Major non-conformance NCR-2027-012 — Calibration traceability

Two reference standards used in final acceptance testing were found to be
outside their calibration interval, one by 47 days.

- Required action: full recall of calibration records for the affected period
- Due: 2027-05-15

## Minor observations

1. Operator training records incomplete for three of eleven assembly staff.
2. Electrostatic discharge wrist-strap testing logged weekly rather than daily.
3. Goods-inward quarantine area not physically segregated.
4. Corrective action closure evidence retained locally rather than in the QMS.

## Capacity and continuity findings

The audit team examined manufacturing capacity and business continuity
arrangements. Alpha Precision Systems operates a single production line for the
inertial navigation product family at the Grenoble site. There is no alternate
production line and no documented site-loss recovery plan for this product
family. The supplier confirmed that reinstating a second production line would
require approximately nine months.

This finding materially reinforces the exposure already carried in the
enterprise risk register in respect of single-source dependency, and the audit
team has recommended that the enterprise risk owner be formally notified.

## Auditor's recommendation

Maintain enhanced incoming inspection. Re-audit within six months. Escalate the
continuity finding to Enterprise Risk Management for reassessment of severity.
