# Project Phoenix — Programme Status Report (April 2027)

Document ID: DOC-STAT-PHOENIX-2027-04
Classification: Internal
Programme director: Elena Vasquez
Reporting period: 2027-04-01 to 2027-04-30

## 1. Overall status

**Status: AMBER**

Project Phoenix remains in Phase C. Schedule performance has deteriorated to
approximately eight weeks behind the January baseline, driven predominantly by
disruption to the inertial navigation supply line. Cost performance remains
within tolerance at 3.1% above the phase budget.

## 2. Schedule

Integration testing on the flight-representative rig was suspended for eleven
working days in February following the quarantine of delivered inertial
navigation units. Testing resumed under a reduced-scope plan using two
previously accepted units held from the development allocation.

The critical path now runs through re-verification of the reverted manufacturing
process and the release of quarantined stock. The programme cannot commit to
recovery of the eight-week slip until that re-verification completes.

## 3. Supply chain

The programme's single greatest exposure is its inertial navigation dependency.
The programme has no qualified alternate part in its baseline and is therefore
fully exposed to the manufacturer's process performance and continuity. Buffer
stock cover currently stands at eleven weeks.

Elena Vasquez has formally requested that the Enterprise Risk Officer reassess
the severity of the associated register entry in light of the March audit
continuity finding.

## 4. Quality

One major incident remains open against the inertial navigation element, with
containment by 100% incoming inspection. One minor incident is open against the
regulated power distribution element with no observed downstream effect.

## 5. Risks and issues

- Supply continuity for the inertial navigation element — carried at enterprise
  level, mitigation overdue.
- Shared dependency on the regulated power distribution element with the launch
  vehicle programme — concentration point raised by the PMO.
- Flight data recorder qualification and delivery — no issues, tracking green.

## 6. Forward look

The programme will hold its integration readiness board in May. A decision on
whether to re-baseline the Phase C exit date will be taken at that board.
