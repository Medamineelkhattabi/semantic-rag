# Helios Aerospace — Contract Register

Document ID: DOC-CTR-2027
Classification: Confidential — Commercial
Owner: Legal & Contracts
Last revised: 2027-04-09

## Scope

The Contract Register records every active commercial instrument, its value,
term, key clauses and the risk entries raised against it during contract review.
Supplier identity is recorded in the Supplier Directory and is deliberately not
duplicated in this register; contracts are managed by instrument number.

## C-2048

- Instrument type: Long-Term Supply Agreement (LTSA)
- Total contract value: EUR 31.4 million
- Effective: 2024-01-01
- Expiry: 2028-12-31
- Payment terms: Net 60
- Liquidated damages: 0.5% of order value per week, capped at 8%
- Termination for convenience: 180 days' notice
- Exclusivity: Supplier holds exclusive manufacturing rights for the qualified
  configuration for the full term

Contract C-2048 has risk R-17 recorded against it. The exclusivity clause and
the absence of a qualified alternate manufacturer were the principal drivers for
raising that risk during the 2026 contract review, and the review board noted
that the 180-day termination notice period offers no practical mitigation
because requalification alone is estimated at 14 months.

## C-3120

- Instrument type: Framework Purchase Agreement
- Total contract value: EUR 9.8 million
- Effective: 2023-06-01
- Expiry: 2027-05-31
- Payment terms: Net 45
- Liquidated damages: 0.3% of order value per week, capped at 5%
- Price adjustment: Indexed to EUR/USD with a 4% collar

Contract C-3120 has risk R-24 recorded against it. The risk was raised because
the price adjustment mechanism transfers currency movement beyond the collar
directly to Helios, and because the instrument expires within the current
financial year without a renewal mandate in place.

## C-4501

- Instrument type: Development and Supply Agreement
- Total contract value: EUR 46.2 million
- Effective: 2025-03-15
- Expiry: 2030-03-14
- Payment terms: Net 30
- Liquidated damages: 0.75% of order value per week, capped at 12%
- Milestone structure: 40% on qualification, 60% on delivery

Contract C-4501 has risk R-31 recorded against it, and additionally has risk
R-08 recorded against it following the 2027 interim review. R-31 was raised in
connection with the cryogenic qualification evidence package; R-08 was raised in
connection with the supplier's financial standing.

## C-5077

- Instrument type: Long-Term Supply Agreement (LTSA)
- Total contract value: EUR 22.9 million
- Effective: 2024-09-01
- Expiry: 2029-08-31
- Payment terms: Net 60
- Liquidated damages: 0.5% of order value per week, capped at 8%
- Export control: Supplier is responsible for obtaining and maintaining all
  export authorisations

Contract C-5077 has risk R-42 recorded against it, reflecting the export-control
dependency created by the clause above.

## Register notes

Risk identifiers recorded in this register are described in full in the Risk
Register, which holds severity, likelihood, mitigation status and risk owner.
This register records the linkage only.
