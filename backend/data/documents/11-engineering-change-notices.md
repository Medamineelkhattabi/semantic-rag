# Helios Aerospace — Engineering Change Notice Log (FY2027)

Document ID: DOC-ECN-2027
Classification: Internal
Owner: Configuration Management
Last revised: 2027-04-14

## Scope

This log records approved and pending engineering change notices affecting
qualified hardware configurations. Each notice identifies the affected component
identifier and the configuration baseline impact.

## ECN-2027-004 — Thermal soak profile reversion

- Raised: 2027-02-14
- Status: Approved
- Affected component: C-17
- Baseline impact: Manufacturing process only, form-fit-function unchanged

Directs reversion of the gyroscope assembly thermal soak profile to the
configuration qualified in 2026, and requires re-verification of bias drift on a
sample of twelve units before the enhanced incoming inspection regime may be
relaxed. Raised in response to the calibration drift incident.

## ECN-2027-006 — Bondline process control

- Raised: 2027-02-01
- Status: Approved
- Affected component: C-58
- Baseline impact: Manufacturing process and acceptance test procedure

Introduces mandatory ultrasonic C-scan of 100% of panels prior to shipment, and
tightens the vacuum bag cure pressure tolerance. Raised in response to the
bondline delamination incident.

## ECN-2027-009 — Adaptive coding firmware update

- Raised: 2027-03-19
- Status: Approved and incorporated
- Affected component: C-45
- Baseline impact: Firmware baseline, hardware unchanged

Incorporates the supplier's corrected adaptive coding loop build resolving the
thermal-load packet loss defect.

## ECN-2027-012 — Cold power-up transient suppression

- Raised: 2027-04-01
- Status: Pending review
- Affected component: C-22
- Baseline impact: Circuit change, requalification required

Proposes an additional transient suppression stage on the primary input rail.
Requalification cost and schedule are under assessment; the change is not yet
approved.

## ECN-2027-015 — Cryogenic cycle life extension test campaign

- Raised: 2027-03-30
- Status: Pending funding
- Affected component: C-31
- Baseline impact: Qualification evidence only, no design change

Proposes an extension of the cycle life test campaign from 400 to 600 cycles to
close the outstanding qualification evidence shortfall. No design change is
proposed; the notice exists to authorise the additional test article and test
facility booking.

## Log notes

Change notices reference component identifiers only. Supplier responsibility for
a component is recorded in the Component Register.
