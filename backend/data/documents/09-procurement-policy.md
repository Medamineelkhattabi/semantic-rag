# Helios Aerospace — Procurement and Sourcing Policy

Document ID: DOC-POL-PROC-2027
Classification: Internal
Owner: Group Procurement
Effective: 2027-01-01
Policy version: 4.2

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the mandatory requirements for supplier selection,
contracting, and ongoing supplier management across all Helios Aerospace
programmes. It applies to every procurement of flight hardware, ground support
equipment and engineering services.

## 2. Sourcing principles

2.1 All flight hardware procurements above EUR 500,000 in aggregate lifetime
value shall be competitively tendered unless a single-source justification is
approved by the Procurement Board.

2.2 Programmes classified as Category 1 (flight-critical) shall maintain a
qualified alternate source for every programme-critical component. Where no
alternate source exists, a formal exception must be recorded and reviewed
annually.

2.3 Exclusivity clauses shall not be accepted in new agreements without written
approval from the Chief Procurement Officer. Legacy agreements containing
exclusivity provisions shall be targeted for renegotiation at renewal.

## 3. Contracting requirements

3.1 Every approved supplier shall operate under exactly one governing
commercial instrument. Purchase orders shall reference that instrument.

3.2 Minimum mandatory clauses: change control and notification, liquidated
damages for late delivery, right of audit, and flow-down of export-control
obligations where applicable.

3.3 Payment terms shall be no shorter than Net 30 and no longer than Net 90.

## 4. Supplier performance management

4.1 Suppliers shall be rated A (Preferred), B (Acceptable, monitored) or
C (Marginal, corrective action required) on a rolling twelve-month basis using
on-time delivery and quality escape rate.

4.2 A supplier rated C for two consecutive quarters shall be placed on a formal
corrective action plan with executive sponsorship.

4.3 Ratings reflect delivery and quality performance only and shall not be
interpreted as an assessment of commercial, financial or regulatory risk.

## 5. Risk interface

5.1 Commercial, financial and regulatory risk arising from a supplier
relationship shall be assessed against the governing contract, not against the
supplier entity, and recorded in the Enterprise Risk Register.

5.2 Any risk assessed as Critical severity shall be escalated to the Audit and
Risk Committee within one reporting cycle.

5.3 Mitigation actions overdue by more than one quarter shall be reported to the
Board.

## 6. Buffer stock and continuity

6.1 Where a programme-critical component has no qualified alternate source, a
strategic buffer stock of not less than six months' consumption shall be
established and maintained.

6.2 Buffer stock coverage shall be reported monthly to the Procurement Board.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Procurement
Officer and shall be recorded in the policy exception log with an expiry date
not exceeding twelve months.
