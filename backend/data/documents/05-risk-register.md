# Helios Aerospace — Enterprise Risk Register

Document ID: DOC-RISK-2027
Classification: Confidential
Owner: Enterprise Risk Management
Last revised: 2027-04-15

## Scope

The Risk Register records every open enterprise-level risk with its severity,
likelihood, mitigation status and accountable owner. Risks are linked to
commercial instruments in the Contract Register; that linkage is not repeated
here.

## R-08 — Supplier financial distress

- Category: Financial
- Severity: High
- Likelihood: Medium
- Risk exposure: EUR 6.4 million
- Status: Open — monitoring
- Risk owner: Yuki Tanaka

A credit review completed in February 2027 downgraded the supplier's rating two
notches following two consecutive loss-making years and a covenant waiver
request to its lending syndicate. Insolvency during the development phase would
strand milestone payments already advanced.

## R-17 — Single-source dependency

- Category: Supply chain
- Severity: Critical
- Likelihood: Medium
- Risk exposure: EUR 14.8 million
- Status: Open — mitigation overdue
- Risk owner: Yuki Tanaka

This risk records the exposure created by dependence on a single qualified
manufacturer for a programme-critical inertial navigation element, compounded by
an exclusivity clause in the governing instrument. Loss of the manufacturer, or
a sustained quality escape, would halt integration testing on the dependent
programme within eleven working days and would require an estimated 14 months to
requalify an alternate source.

Mitigation actions:

1. Reinstate the suspended second-source qualification programme — status:
   NOT STARTED, originally due 2026-09-30, now 28 weeks overdue.
2. Establish a six-month strategic buffer stock — status: PARTIAL, currently
   holding eleven weeks of cover against a six-month target.
3. Negotiate removal of the exclusivity clause at contract renewal — status:
   NOT STARTED.

R-17 is the highest-severity open supply chain risk in the FY2027 register and
was escalated to the Board in April 2027.

## R-24 — Currency and price-adjustment exposure

- Category: Commercial
- Severity: Medium
- Likelihood: High
- Risk exposure: EUR 1.1 million
- Status: Open — mitigation in progress
- Risk owner: Yuki Tanaka

Movement in the EUR/USD rate beyond the contractual collar passes directly to
Helios. The exposure is bounded but recurring, and the governing instrument
expires within the financial year without an agreed renewal position.

## R-31 — Cryogenic qualification evidence gap

- Category: Technical / compliance
- Severity: High
- Likelihood: Medium
- Risk exposure: EUR 3.9 million
- Status: Open — corrective action in progress
- Risk owner: Priya Raghunathan

The qualification evidence package for cryogenic service is incomplete: cycle
life testing was performed to 400 cycles against a 600-cycle requirement, and
the shortfall was accepted on a conditional basis pending completion. Regulatory
acceptance of the conditional position is not assured.

## R-42 — Export control and licensing

- Category: Regulatory
- Severity: High
- Likelihood: Low
- Risk exposure: EUR 5.2 million
- Status: Open — monitoring
- Risk owner: Anna Kowalski

The governing instrument places responsibility for export authorisations on the
supplier. A licence lapse, revocation or policy change in the supplier's
jurisdiction would interrupt delivery with no contractual remedy available to
Helios beyond liquidated damages.

## Register notes

Risk owners are accountable for mitigation delivery. The linkage between a risk
and the commercial instrument it was raised against is held in the Contract
Register; the linkage between an instrument and the supplier it governs is held
in the Supplier Directory.
