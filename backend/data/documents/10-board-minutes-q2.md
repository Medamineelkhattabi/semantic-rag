# Audit and Risk Committee — Minutes of the Q2 2027 Meeting

Document ID: DOC-BOARD-2027Q2
Classification: Confidential — Board
Date: 2027-04-22
Location: Toulouse (hybrid)

Present: Committee chair, three non-executive directors, Chief Financial
Officer, Chief Procurement Officer, Enterprise Risk Officer (Yuki Tanaka),
Head of Quality Assurance.

## 1. Minutes of the previous meeting

Approved without amendment.

## 2. Quarterly risk position

The Enterprise Risk Officer presented the FY2027 register position. Five risks
remain open. One risk is rated Critical severity; the remainder are rated High
or Medium.

The Committee noted with concern that the Critical-rated supply chain risk
carries a mitigation action that is now 28 weeks overdue, and that a second
mitigation action remains only partially delivered at eleven weeks of buffer
cover against a six-month policy requirement. The Committee observed that this
constitutes a standing breach of section 6.1 of the procurement policy.

**Resolution 2027-Q2-04:** The Chief Procurement Officer is directed to present
a funded second-source qualification plan to the Committee no later than the Q3
meeting. The Committee will not accept a further deferral.

## 3. Supplier quality escalation

The Head of Quality Assurance summarised the outcome of the March supplier audit
at the Grenoble facility, reporting two major non-conformances and a continuity
finding of single-line production with no documented site-loss recovery plan.

The Committee agreed that the continuity finding compounds the existing
Critical-rated exposure and asked that severity be reassessed at the next
register review rather than waiting for the annual cycle.

## 4. Contract renewals

The Committee noted that one framework instrument expires within the current
financial year without an agreed renewal position, and that the associated
currency exposure risk remains open with mitigation in progress. The Chief
Financial Officer confirmed that the bounded exposure is within tolerance and no
provision is required.

## 5. Programme concentration

The Committee reviewed the PMO's observation that a single regulated power
distribution component is shared between the two largest development
programmes, creating a concentration point. The Chief Procurement Officer
confirmed that the supplier of that component holds an A rating and that a
qualified second source exists for the Aurora dual-source mandate but not for
the shared power component.

**Action 2027-Q2-07:** PMO to model the schedule impact of a twelve-week
interruption in supply of the shared power component across both affected
programmes.

## 6. Any other business

None.

## 7. Date of next meeting

Q3 2027, date to be confirmed.
