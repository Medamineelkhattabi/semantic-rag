# Helios Aerospace — Approved Supplier Directory

Document ID: DOC-SUPP-2027
Classification: Internal — Commercial in Confidence
Owner: Group Procurement
Last revised: 2027-04-11

## Scope

This directory lists every supplier currently on the approved vendor list, with
its legal entity details, performance rating and the contractual instrument
under which the relationship operates. Component allocations are held in the
Component Register and are not repeated here.

## SUP-ALPHA — Alpha Precision Systems

Alpha Precision Systems is a precision inertial-sensing manufacturer based in
Grenoble, France, employing approximately 480 staff across two sites.

- Legal entity: Alpha Precision Systems S.A.S.
- Registered office: Grenoble, France
- Relationship since: 2019
- On-time delivery (rolling 12 months): 91.4%
- Quality escape rate: 340 ppm
- Supplier performance rating: B (Acceptable, monitored)

The relationship with Alpha Precision Systems is governed by contract C-2048.
All purchase orders raised against Alpha Precision Systems must reference
contract C-2048 as the governing instrument. Procurement responsibility for this
supplier sits with Samuel Okonkwo.

## Directory notes

A supplier's performance rating reflects delivery and quality history only. It
does not incorporate commercial, financial or regulatory risk exposure, which is
assessed against the governing contract and recorded in the Risk Register. Users
seeking the risk profile of a supplier relationship must first identify the
governing contract in this directory and then consult the Contract Register.
